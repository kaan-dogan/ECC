# Safe Research

Research the requested provider, API, or integration. Separate verified facts from assumptions, capture relevant current constraints, identify compatibility and security concerns, and recommend a smallest safe experiment.

Do not authenticate, install software, change configuration, make purchases, send messages, or call production APIs. Return links and a concise decision brief only.
