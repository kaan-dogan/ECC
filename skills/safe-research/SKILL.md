# Safe Research

Prefer official, current sources. Report what is confirmed, what remains unknown, the relevant constraints and risks, and the smallest safe experiment.

Do not authenticate, install software, change configuration, purchase anything, send messages, or call production APIs. Return a concise decision brief only.
